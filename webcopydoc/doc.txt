ABOUT PAGE
My Story
I’ve always loved people.
Their stories.
Their ideas.
Their dreams.
The moments when they know something needs to change, but aren’t quite sure what comes next.
Over the years, I’ve been a witness to thousands of these moments.
My career has taken me from live events and agency life to leading marketing and brand experiences for an international salon franchise to working for myself and living the life I love. Along the way, I’ve worked with passionate founders, entrepreneurs, creatives, stylists and leaders building businesses they care deeply about.
And somewhere along the way, I realized I was navigating my own life and transitions too.
From corporate executive to entrepreneur.
From workaholic to boundaried.
From double-income-no-kids to motherhood.
From constantly doing to learning how to simply be.
Some of those transitions were exciting.
Some were messy and painful.
Most were both.
I’ve collected wisdom from my mentors, coaches, therapists, shamans, retreats, life-changing books, challenging bosses, incredible teams, sleepless nights, and the beautifully imperfect experience of being human. 
It's why I believe so deeply that we aren't meant to navigate life and business alone. 
Today, my work brings all of those experiences together.
Part strategy.
Part reflection.
Part coaching.
Part conversation.
A space to slow down, think clearly, and move forward with confidence.
What I Believe
Growth rarely follows a straight line. Small, consistent actions create meaningful change. 
Boundaries create freedom. Burnout is a terrible long-term strategy.
You don’t have to carry everything alone.
And clarity changes everything. 
When I’m Not Working
You’ll usually find me with my feet in the grass at Wash Park, taking a long walk or hike with my pup, practicing yoga, listening to music, catching a live show, gathering with friends and family, chasing my two little humans around, or simply breathing in the sunshine.
I believe wellness is worth prioritizing. Connection matters. And joy belongs in the everyday moments.
